# Clone-match notes

The visual/motion layer in `dist/` is matched to the supplied HTTrack clone of the deployed Buddy Computers site.

Intentional differences from the clone:
- supplied Buddy Computers logo/favicon assets are used;
- the large footer `Buddy Computers` wordmark is constrained to remain inside the viewport;
- the floating WhatsApp button is lifted above footer links/content.

The reference cinematic assets under `dist/assets/cine/` are included because the cloned pages depend on them.
